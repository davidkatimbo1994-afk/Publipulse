# District Weekly Outcome Review

## Purpose

The weekly review is an operational decision workflow for authorized district/programme teams. It is not a surveillance dashboard, outbreak classifier, clinical decision tool, or causal-effect estimator.

## Review agenda

1. **Ownership gaps** — signals that did not reach an accountable owner.
2. **Response decay** — where verified work drops before action or measured outcome.
3. **Unresolved outcomes** — completed/closed work without sufficient outcome evidence.
4. **Recurrence** — comparable observations after previous action.
5. **Persistent/intervention-resistant patterns** — recurring patterns requiring a structured human decision.
6. **Resource blockers** — normalized operational constraints preventing response.
7. **Reporter feedback** — whether source feedback was attempted and delivered.
8. **Outcome learning** — what changed, what remains uncertain, and what should be reviewed next week.

## Privacy boundary

District aggregates are shown only when the signal count meets the configured minimum-cell threshold. Individual cases are not included in the aggregate review response. Suppressed values are not converted to zero.

## Required weekly decisions

For persistent patterns, the reviewer records one of:

- continue current approach;
- change approach;
- escalate for programme redesign;
- insufficient evidence.

These are operational decisions, not claims of intervention efficacy or causation.

## Pilot evidence

A pilot should retain the weekly review artifact and record which decisions changed because of the review. This provides evidence for implementation evaluation without claiming that PubliPulse caused health outcomes.
