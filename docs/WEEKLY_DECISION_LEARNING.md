# Weekly Decision & Learning Record

PubliPulse records the human decision made from a district/programme weekly review and the next checkpoint.

## Workflow

1. Review the aggregate weekly outcome/accountability summary.
2. Select the agenda issue (ownership, unresolved outcome, recurrence, blocker, intervention-resistant pattern, feedback gap, or integration gap).
3. Record one bounded decision: continue current approach, change approach, escalate for programme redesign, or insufficient evidence.
4. Record a short rationale without direct identifiers.
5. Assign an owner role and a next-check date.
6. Review the decision at the next checkpoint and create a new append-only record if the decision changes.

A decision record is operational learning evidence. It does not prove causation, clinical effectiveness, outbreak control, or programme superiority.

## Data boundary

The rationale is checked for common direct identifiers. The record stores no patient/client/person identifiers. Evidence is referenced by bounded digest rather than copied narrative.
